MODULE 01 · VISUAL DISINFORMATION

Frame-by-Frame Video Deepfake Inspection

Where generative video models and face swaps fail physics, human anatomy, and lighting consistency.

Ocular Test 15s Quick Check

Unnatural Blinking & Static Pupil Reflections

Key Forensic Tell

Look closely at the white highlights inside the pupils. In deepfakes, specular corneal reflections remain static or asymmetric as the subject's head rotates.

The Science

Humans blink naturally every 2 to 6 seconds. Budget generative models frequently produce zero blinking or sudden, single-frame flickers due to lack of closed-eye training imagery.

Inspection Procedure

Pause footage on a tight close-up and match pupil reflection highlights to the room light sources. Real reflections obey strict optics; AI composites mismatch the ambient light angles.

Labial Test 0.5x Slow-Mo

Lip-Sync Latency & Solid "Teeth Bar"

Key Forensic Tell

Play at 0.5x speed. Real speech requires lips to seal shut on "B", "P", and "M". Deepfakes keep lips slightly parted, and teeth often blur into a solid white block.

The Science

Speech-to-video alignment engines struggle with plosive consonants that require high-velocity mechanical closures. Rendered mouth interiors blur dental separation into an undifferentiated white strip.

Inspection Procedure

Slow playback to 0.5x. Watch for words like "billion", "people", or "press". If the consonant sound plays while lips remain open, synthetic synchronization is confirmed.

Peripheral Test Edge Boundary

Melting Geometry & Boundary Warping

Key Forensic Tell

Watch glasses arms, earrings, collars, and sideburns. Face-swap models blend the central facial triangle, leaving boundary melting and jitter when the head turns.

The Science

Autoencoders blend the synthesized face onto the original neck using a feathered boundary mask. Thin linear structures crossing this boundary momentarily dissolve during movement.

Inspection Procedure

Track spectacle frames or earrings frame-by-frame. In deepfakes, spectacle arms will momentarily dissolve into cheek skin or necklace links will lose continuity.

Photometric Test Shadow Vectors

Conflicting Light Angles on Neck & Scene

Key Forensic Tell

Compare the nose and chin shadow angles with background light sources. Composite deepfakes routinely place studio-lit faces into outdoor scenes with conflicting shadow physics.

The Science

Synthetic training datasets rely on diverse lighting conditions. When pasted into video, synthetic faces lack secondary bounce lighting from nearby clothing or environmental surfaces.

Inspection Procedure

If background sunlight is on the subject's right, but the shadow under their nose points left, the face has been composited from unrelated training data.

MODULE 02 · ACOUSTIC INTELLIGENCE

Synthetic Voice & Audio Clone Forensics

WhatsApp voice notes are the primary vector for political hoaxes, banking fraud, and emergency extortion scams.

Acoustic Test Pulmonary Airflow

The Physiological Breath Gap

Key Forensic Tell

Listen closely for pulmonary inhalation. If an urgent audio note delivers a 40-word complex sentence without a single audible breath or micro-pause, suspect AI synthesis.

The Science

Human phonation requires lung pressure. Natural speech contains biological micro-intakes of air, vocal cord resets, and saliva clicks between clauses.

Inspection Procedure

Use headphones and isolate transitions between phrases. Generative TTS engines (ElevenLabs) generate continuous phonemes from text tokens without natural breathing intervals unless explicitly prompted.

Prosodic Test Pitch Variance

Prosodic Flattening & Corporate Cadence

Key Forensic Tell

During crises, real human voices spike with adrenaline and tremor. AI voice clones maintain a smooth, uniform podcast cadence even when reporting catastrophic emergency events.

The Science

Biological fight-or-flight activates sympathetic vocal cord tension. AI voice models struggle to replicate authentic jitter and emotional pitch instability without sounding robotic.

Inspection Procedure

Listen for emotional dissonance: panic claims delivered with the cheerful, measured diction of a commercial studio narrator.

Ambience Test Noise Floor

Background Noise Discontinuity

Key Forensic Tell

Listen to the background room noise during speech pauses. Fake voice notes often superimpose stock siren or crowd audio that abruptly cuts out between spoken sentences.

The Science

Attackers generate speech in a silent software synthesizer, then overlay ambient background noise using basic video editing apps. The resulting audio gate frequently clamps ambient sound.

Inspection Procedure

Turn volume to high during pauses. In genuine field recordings, ambient room reverb sustains continuously; in synthetic audio, background hiss resets on sentence cuts.

MODULE 03 · GRAPHIC & DOCUMENT AUTOPSY

Manipulated Photos & Fake Government Circulars

How to expose fabricated news tickers, doctored gazette orders, and altered viral infographics.

Typography Test Font Kerning

Font Weight & Antialiasing Mismatch

Key Forensic Tell

Broadcasters (NDTV, BBC, TV9) use locked broadcast fonts. Forgeries use default system fonts (Arial, Calibri) with fuzzy edge halos against crisp TV channel graphics.

The Science

Broadcast title-generation engines rasterize text with sub-pixel rendering. Pasted edits introduce double-compression artifacts and misalignment along baseline vectors.

Inspection Procedure

Zoom in 300% on the headline. If letters have gray blur halos while the channel logo is razor sharp, the lower-third text was added in an image editor.

Official Gazette Dispatch Log

Missing Tracking File Reference Number

Key Forensic Tell

Every genuine government circular (RBI, Education Ministry) requires a formal tracking dispatch number (e.g. F.No. 12/2026/Fin-Sec). Circulars lacking this are fabricated.

The Science

Official public notices follow strict administrative archiving mandates including dispatch numbers, bilingual headers, and registered signatories.

Inspection Procedure

Copy the file tracking reference and search it on the official ministry portal (.gov.in or .nic.in). If no official record exists in the public gazette, the circular is fake.

Rhetorical Test Viral Triggers

Panic Phrasing & Forwarding Demands

Key Forensic Tell

Legitimate news reports facts neutrally. Fake graphics add panic hooks: "Forward to all family groups before 12 AM!" or "Mainstream media will never broadcast this!".

The Science

Viral disinformation exploits emotional urgency to trigger sharing before critical skepticism engages. Authentic institutions never mandate forwarding quotas.

Inspection Procedure

Scan the text for countdown deadlines or calls to circulate immediately. Emotional panic hooks are the #1 hallmark of manufactured propaganda.

METHODOLOGY · DIGITAL LITERACY

The 4-Step S.I.F.T. Verification Protocol

Developed by digital literacy scholar Mike Caulfield, this is the gold-standard protocol used by professional fact-checkers worldwide.

Step 01 Immediate Pause

S — STOP

The Core Action

When a post triggers an immediate surge of outrage, fear, or excitement, pause. Stop your finger before tapping share.

The Principle

Disinformation is engineered to hijack your emotional nervous system before logical skepticism engages. Pausing for 30 seconds interrupts the viral reflex.

Step 02 Lateral Reading

I — INVESTIGATE THE SOURCE

The Core Action

Who published this? Check account handle for typos or extra underscores (e.g. @BBCNews_Official_). Search the author externally.

The Principle

Never rely on an unfamiliar website's own "About Us" page. Practice lateral reading: open a new tab and search what accredited news organizations say about this publisher.

Step 03 Consensus Search

F — FIND BETTER COVERAGE

The Core Action

If a monumental world event occurred, every major wire (Reuters, AP, PTI, BBC) will cover it within minutes—not just a random Telegram channel.

The Principle

Search the core keywords of the claim. Look for established consensus across multiple independent newsrooms rather than a single viral graphic.

Step 04 First Upload

T — TRACE CLAIMS TO ORIGINAL CONTEXT

The Core Action

Trace footage or images to their original upload. Most viral "crimes" turn out to be clips from old movies, emergency drills, or different countries.

The Principle

Disinformation often takes authentic, real video footage and weaponizes it by attaching an entirely fabricated false caption or date.
