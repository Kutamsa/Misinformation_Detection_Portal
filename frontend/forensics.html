MODULE 01 · VISUAL DISINFORMATION

Frame-by-Frame Video Deepfake Autopsy

Where generative video models (face swaps, text-to-video) consistently fail physics and human biology.

👁️ THE OCULAR TEST Blinking & Corneal Physics

Unnatural Blinking & Static Pupil Reflections

The Science: Humans blink naturally every 2 to 6 seconds. Early and budget deepfakes often show zero blinking or hyper-erratic flickering.

How to inspect: Look at the white highlight (specular reflection) inside the eye. In real footage, reflections match the room's light sources. In deepfakes, eye reflections are often asymmetric or static when the head moves.

👄 THE LABIAL TEST Plosive Consonants (B, P, M)

Lip-Sync Latency & Solid "Teeth Bar"

The Science: Deepfake lip-sync models struggle with plosive sounds like "B", "P", and "M", which physically require upper and lower lips to seal shut.

How to inspect: Slow the video to 0.5x speed. In synthetic audio-driven video, words like "billion" or "people" will be heard while the lips remain slightly open. Teeth also frequently appear as a single blurry white bar without distinct gaps.

👂 PERIPHERAL TEST Ears, Glasses & Hair Strands

Melting Geometry & Boundary Warping

The Science: AI face-swap models focus on the central facial triangle (eyes, nose, mouth) and blend it into an existing neck.

How to inspect: Ignore the center of the face. Watch the jawline, earrings, glasses frames, and collar. In deepfakes, spectacle arms will momentarily dissolve into hair, or earrings will lose symmetry when the speaker turns their head.

💡 PHOTOMETRIC TEST Lighting & Shadow Conflicts

Conflicting Light Sources on Neck & Background

The Science: Synthetic faces are often extracted from bright studio photos and pasted onto outdoor or dim footage.

How to inspect: If the sun is visibly on the subject's left in the background, but the shadow under their nose points to the left as well, the footage is mathematically composited.

MODULE 02 · ACOUSTIC INTELLIGENCE

Synthetic Voice & Audio Clone Forensics

WhatsApp voice forwards are the #1 attack vector for political, banking, and emergency scams.

🫁

1. The Physiological Breath Gap

Human speech requires pulmonary airflow. We naturally inhale between phrases, adjust saliva pressure, and produce micro-pauses. AI voice clones (ElevenLabs, Coqui) often generate continuous, uninterrupted speech without natural diaphragmatic inhalation.

Rule: If someone speaks a complex 40-word paragraph without a single audible breath, suspect AI synthesis.
📉

2. Prosodic Flattening & Emotional Monotone

When real people share urgent news, their vocal pitch fluctuates dynamically with adrenaline, skepticism, or surprise. AI voice clones maintain a suspiciously steady cadence and uniform pitch, sounding eerily polished even during supposed crisis situations.

Rule: Listen for emotional dissonance—urgent claims delivered in smooth corporate podcast cadence.
📻

3. Background Noise Discontinuity

If someone claims to be recording an urgent voice note from a hospital, railway station, or protest, listen closely to the ambient background. Cloned audio frequently layers artificial looped background noise that abruptly starts and cuts off with each sentence.

Rule: Check if background room reverberation stays uniform when the speaker pauses.
MODULE 03 · GRAPHIC & DOCUMENT AUTOPSY

Manipulated Screenshots & Fake Government Circulars

How to catch fabricated channel tickers, altered RBI/bank circulars, and fake viral social posts.

🔤 TYPOGRAPHY CLASH

Font Weight & Antialiasing Mismatch

Broadcasters (BBC, Aaj Tak, NDTV, TV9) use locked vector broadcast font suites. Forgers use Photoshop or Canva to type over lower-thirds, leaving telltale signs: default Arial/Calibri fonts, uneven character baseline heights, and blurry text against sharp graphics.

🏛️ OFFICIAL GAZETTES

The File Reference Number Protocol

Every legitimate government circular (RBI, Education Dept, Ministry of Home Affairs) contains an official tracking file number (e.g. F.No. 12/2026/Fin-Sec) and bilingual letterheads. If a viral viral order lacks an official dispatch number, it is 99% fabricated.

🚩 URGENCY SIGNALS

Panic Phrasing & Call to Action

Real news organizations report facts calmly. Fake news graphics always inject emotional call-to-action hooks: "Forward to all family groups before 12 AM!", "Media will never show this!", or "Alert your children immediately!".

METHODOLOGY

The 4-Step S.I.F.T. Verification Protocol

Developed by digital literacy scholar Mike Caulfield, this is the gold-standard protocol used by professional fact-checkers worldwide.

S

STOP

When you feel a sudden surge of outrage, fear, or vindication, stop immediately. Disinformation is specifically engineered to hijack your emotional nervous system before your logic engages.

I

INVESTIGATE SOURCE

Who published this? Does the account have an impersonation handle with extra underscores (e.g., @BBC_News_India_)? Does the website have an editorial address or journalistic masthead?

F

FIND BETTER COVERAGE

Look for consensus. If a major world event or catastrophic emergency actually happened, every major news wire (Reuters, AP, PTI, BBC) will report it within minutes, not just a random Telegram channel.

T

TRACE TO ORIGINAL

Trace the claim or media back to its original uncropped context. Most viral video "crimes" turn out to be clips from 2017 music videos, mock drills, or completely unrelated incidents from other countries.
