How to Identify AI-Generated Images & Deepfakes

As generative AI tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, Sora) become common, use this practical 4-point inspection checklist to spot synthetic media.

✋

1. Hand & Finger Anatomy

AI image models frequently struggle with fine motor geometry. Look for:

  • Extra, missing, or conjoined fingers
  • Unnatural finger lengths and deformed joints
  • Melted fingernails or rings that blend into the skin
Common in Midjourney / Flux
👁️

2. Eye Catchlights & Pupils

In authentic photography, light reflections in both pupils must match the scene's light sources:

  • Asymmetrical or non-circular pupil shapes
  • Mismatched catchlights (e.g. square reflection in one eye, dot in another)
  • Blurred boundaries between the iris and the sclera (white of eye)
Key Clue in Portrait Deepfakes
🔤

3. Background Text & Gibberish

Diffusion models often cannot render coherent typography in backgrounds:

  • Pseudo-English or gibberish symbols on street signs and shirts
  • Warped logos on branded apparel or police badges
  • Nonsensical numbers on license plates or building placards
Dead Giveaway on Fake Posters
🔍

4. Reverse Image Search (Google Lens)

Trace the image back to its original creator before sharing viral graphics:

  • Upload to Google Lens or TinEye to find the earliest publication date
  • Look for labels like "Created by AI Artist" on Reddit or ArtStation
  • Check if the photo only appeared within the last 2 hours without credible news coverage
Verification Gold Standard
